# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Steward primarily serves short-term-rental owners and operators with roughly one to twenty properties. They need incidents resolved without staying awake to coordinate guests and vendors. Vendors use a permanent role-specific workspace to receive jobs, communicate, provide evidence, and see payment status. Guests are temporary participants: they enter through a booking-scoped link, report a problem, talk with Steward, and share their camera only when visual evidence is genuinely useful.

## Product Purpose

Steward is an autonomous property manager that carries a guest incident from first report to verified resolution. It talks naturally, reasons from real voice and visual input, troubleshoots before spending, contacts approved vendors before searching elsewhere, compares options, acts within the owner's budget, verifies the outcome, and creates externally visible proof instead of merely claiming success.

Success means a guest receives a real resolution, the owner can see what happened and why money was spent, the vendor has a clear job and payment record, and every important claim is backed by a tool result or evidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, accountable.

Steward should feel human under pressure, precise around money and access, and quietly confident rather than theatrical. Its voice and interface use concise language and reveal complexity only when it helps someone act.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS pages made from repeated cards, glowing gradients, glass panels, chatbot mascots, and vague claims.
- Crypto-style command centers that use darkness, neon, and dense metrics as decoration rather than information.
- Wordy enterprise pages that bury the primary action in marketing copy.
- A direct recreation of Apple's product, typography, imagery, or page choreography. Apple Vision Pro is only an inspiration for spatial restraint and high-quality 3D presentation.
- Interfaces that request the guest's camera immediately, pretend every guest needs an account, or expose owner/vendor controls to temporary guests.

## Design Principles

1. **The voice is the product.** The interface supports the call and the outcome; it does not compete with them.
2. **Show evidence, not confidence.** Display real call, tool, quote, verification, and payment states instead of decorative activity.
3. **One identity, clear roles.** Owners and vendors may share an identity, but their workspaces, permissions, and primary actions remain distinct.
4. **Earn every interruption.** Ask for camera access, attention, or confirmation only when the current incident needs it.
5. **Spatial storytelling with operational clarity.** The landing page can be visually ambitious and three-dimensional; authenticated product surfaces remain focused, legible, and fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the functional product. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, captions or transcripts for voice state, and clear non-color status cues. Provide reduced-motion and static fallbacks for 3D and animated surfaces without using accessibility as a reason to weaken the primary visual experience.
